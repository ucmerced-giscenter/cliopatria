For work using these boundaries, cite the aligned release and the original research. Record your selected years and keep the downloaded data with your analysis. The suggested aligned citation below is awaiting final approval by the data owner.

Copy or download citations

Copy all three references together, or save a citation file for your reference manager.

Aligned UC Merced release

GIS Center and Geospatial Services, UC Merced University Library. (2026). Aligned Cliopatria (v1.1) [Data set]. Aligned and reviewed by Erin Mutch. https://ucmerced.maps.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=8eae64c12ac047d3933d43e449c8e551

Original research

Bennett, J. S., Mutch, E., Tollefson, A., et al. (2025). Cliopatria – A geospatial database of world-wide political entities from 3400BCE to 2024CE. Scientific Data, 12, 247. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04516-9

Upstream dataset version

Chalstrey, E., Bennett, J., & Mutch, E. (2024). Cliopatria – A geospatial database of world-wide political entities from 3400BCE to 2024CE (v0.0.1) [Data set]. Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13363121

The upstream dataset's title covers 3400 BCE–2024 CE. The aligned release in this map currently ends at 1497 CE. The upstream DOI identifies the source dataset; it is not a DOI for the aligned derivative.
