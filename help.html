Timeline instructions

The timeline displays one historical snapshot at a time. This release contains 333 available snapshots from 3400 BCE to 1497 CE. Each slider step represents an available snapshot; the number of calendar years between steps varies.

Choose a year

  1. Use the Year menu to choose an available snapshot directly, or drag the slider to move through the available years.
  2. Use Previous and Next to move one snapshot at a time. For example, Next from 117 CE moves to 127 CE. These controls are disabled at the first and last snapshots.
  3. Read the Year menu and the map's year label to confirm the displayed year. The snapshot counter shows your position in the sequence, not how many calendar years have passed.

Open the map at 117 CE to try the controls. Only polygons belonging to the selected snapshot appear; the app does not blend boundaries or invent intermediate years.

BCE, CE, and missing years

BCE dates come before CE dates. In numeric fields and shared links, BCE years use a minus sign: -3400 means 3400 BCE, and 117 means 117 CE. There is no year zero. If a shared link requests a year absent from the dataset, the map selects the nearest available snapshot; check the displayed year after opening it.

Play and pause

Press Play to advance through snapshots at about two-second intervals. Playback waits for the map data to load, so a slow connection can make it take longer. Press Pause to stop at the current snapshot. Playback does not start automatically.

Playback stops at the final snapshot, when you choose a year manually, select a feature, begin using search, or leave the browser tab. Pressing Play while already at the final snapshot starts again from the first snapshot.

What changes with the year

Changing the year clears the selected feature and its popup. An existing Map year search refreshes for the new snapshot. An All years search keeps its results, while the map highlights only matches in the displayed year. Selecting a search result from a different year switches the map to that result's snapshot. See Search instructions.

For downloads using Map year, the year displayed when you press Prepare download determines the export. Custom year ranges and All years use their own time settings. Changing the timeline afterward does not change an export already being prepared. To download several snapshots, use a custom year range in Download data. See Download instructions.

Keyboard, touch, and sharing

Use Tab to reach the Year menu, slider, and buttons. Arrow keys move the focused slider through snapshots; Previous and Next are alternatives to dragging on a small screen. Press Enter or Space to activate a focused button.

Copy map link copies the current year, map location, zoom, basemap, label setting, and submitted search. It does not preserve a selected feature or download settings. Open the link to return to that map view.

Search instructions

Use Search by name to find a polity or another named feature in the source dataset. Search is case-insensitive and matches part of a name: “Roman” can find Roman Empire and other names containing that word.

Search with suggested names

  1. Choose Map year to search the snapshot displayed on the map, or All years to search every available snapshot in this release.
  2. Click, tap, or Tab into Polity name to see up to six names in alphabetical order, even before typing. Suggestions use the same Map year or All years setting.
  3. Type to narrow the suggestions from the first character. Matching names update after a short pause. Delete the text to return to the alphabetical list.
  4. Choose a suggested name to fill the field and run the search. You can also keep typing and press Enter or Search. Suggestions are optional and are not the complete result list.
  5. Select a result to show its details and outline its polygon. When searching all years, selecting a result also changes the map to that result's snapshot.

Try searching for Roman in 117 CE. Change Search within to All years and press Search to include other snapshots.

Read and refine results

Results load in groups of 25; Show more results adds another group. Each result lists its Name, Year, Type, and area when supplied. One name can appear in many years, and several separate polygons can share the same Name and Year. Check the year and area before selecting a record.

Blue outlines mark matching features in the current map year. The white and dark outline marks the selected feature; its details also appear in the Selected feature panel. Results from other years remain in the list when All years is selected, but only the current snapshot appears on the map.

Changing the map year refreshes an existing Map year search. After changing the search text or Search within setting, choose a suggestion or press Search to replace the submitted search. Clear removes search results and their blue outlines; Clear selection separately removes the selected feature.

If you cannot find a feature

  • Try a shorter part of the name, another spelling, or All years. Suggestions show only a few matching names; Search can return additional matches.
  • Leave the name blank and press Search to browse features, including intentionally unnamed regions. Suggestions contain named features only and show a small starting list; they are not the full dataset.
  • Search uses the Name field. Dedicated alternate-name and region searches are not available. The characters % and _ act as database wildcards.
  • If suggestions are unavailable, the Search button still works when the query service is reachable. If the search itself fails, check your connection and try again.

Keyboard and touch

Tap or click a suggested name. With a keyboard, use Tab to reach a suggestion, or press Down Arrow from the name field. Up and Down move between suggestions; Up from the first returns to the name field. Enter or Space activates a focused suggestion. Escape dismisses suggestions and returns focus to the name field. Enter in the name field runs the typed search.

Select directly or download results

You can also select a polygon directly on the map. Where features overlap, use the Overlapping features menu. Area is the publisher's AreaSqKM value. A supplied Wikipedia article opens in another tab; some features have no article or name.

To export a search, choose Current search results in Download data. It includes every match from the last submitted search, including unloaded result pages. To export one record, select it and choose Use selected feature in the download section. See download instructions.

Download instructions

  1. Choose Year or year range, Current search results, Visible map extent, Selected feature, or Entire dataset.
  2. For a year range or map extent, choose Map year, Custom year range, or All years. Ranges include every available panel between their endpoints. For example, -3400 through -1 includes BCE panels. 1000 through 1497 includes this release's most recent approximately 500 years.
  3. Choose GeoJSON, CSV, KMZ, or Shapefile, then Prepare download.
  4. When the file is ready, use the Download link. No ArcGIS account is required for this public layer.

Visible extent uses the map rectangle when you press Prepare download. It includes every polygon intersecting that rectangle in the chosen period, keeping each polygon whole. It does not clip borders or export a picture of the map. Panning afterward does not change the pending export.

Search exports include all matching results, including results beyond the first page. Entire dataset exports contain all years, irrespective of the displayed year. Polygons from different snapshots can overlap in a multi-year export; use Year when analyzing them.

ArcGIS prepares the file and supplies a temporary download link. Large exports may take a minute or longer. Keep the page open while it prepares. Stop waiting stops this page's progress checks; it does not cancel the server job. Generate the file again if its link expires. The browser saves the file without the app reading it all into memory.

Choosing a format
FormatContents and limitations
GeoJSONGeometry and attributes in longitude/latitude (WGS84). Recommended for preserving the source fields, including OBJECTID.
CSVAttribute table; no polygon geometry.
KMZCompressed KML for Google Earth. The current service exports a uniform blue style and blank placemark names; Name and Year remain in feature descriptions. This does not reproduce the web map's color styling.
Shapefile ZIPGeometry and DBF attributes. Current ArcGIS exports omit the original OBJECTID. Keep all component files together after extracting the ZIP.

A very large selection crossing the antimeridian (180° longitude) may be prepared in numbered parts to stay within ArcGIS query limits. Download every part for the complete selection. Full-dataset and year-range exports remain single files. If preparation is stopped, links already shown contain only the completed parts.

OBJECTID can change between releases. The publisher refers to Name + Year as a stable reference, but multiple polygons can share those values; they are not a unique row key in this layer. Preserve your original files and access date, especially for unnamed regions. See citation instructions.

Keyboard and mobile access

Use Tab to move between controls and Enter or Space to activate buttons. Arrow keys adjust the focused year slider. The year menu is an alternative to dragging. Search results provide a text route to selection, details, and downloads without clicking a polygon. On small screens, the map appears above the controls; scroll down for search and downloads.

The map needs a WebGL-capable browser and an internet connection for basemaps, search, and exports. If the map cannot render, search and downloads remain available. The Plain background option reduces basemap traffic. Labels appear as you zoom in.

Privacy and service availability

The map page includes Plausible analytics and download-link click tracking. A click does not confirm that a file finished downloading. No account sign-in is required, and the app does not keep a persistent dataset cache. Requests for basemaps, searches, and downloads go to Esri's services. Normal browser and map-library caches may be used.
